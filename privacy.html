Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026

This policy explains what the Orbit Roblox Launcher processes when you use it. Orbit is a personal launcher interface; Roblox handles your Roblox account sign-in and game sessions.

Information Orbit processes

If you choose Connect Roblox, Orbit requests the OpenID Connect openid and profile scopes. After Roblox authorizes the request, Orbit receives basic profile information such as your Roblox user ID, username, display name, and, if provided, profile image URL. Orbit does not request your Roblox password.

Orbit exchanges the one-time authorization code on its server. The access token is used to request your profile and is not saved in the browser or Orbit's session. Orbit stores the returned basic profile in an encrypted, HttpOnly session cookie for up to seven days. A separate encrypted OAuth state and PKCE verifier cookie is temporary and expires after ten minutes.

Your Orbit library can save up to 30 Roblox place IDs, tile names, and color choices in this browser. Orbit also stores up to five recently opened place IDs, your selected theme and home-section visibility/order, and up to 1,000 characters of notes. These settings and notes stay in browser local storage; Orbit does not send them to its server. They are not encrypted and may be visible to anyone with access to this browser profile. Remove games in Orbit or clear this site's browser storage to erase them.

When you search by game name, the search text is sent to Orbit's Vercel function and forwarded to Roblox's public search service. Orbit caches up to ten recent result sets in this browser for five minutes so repeat searches can appear immediately. When you open a game's information view, Orbit requests public Roblox experience details, recommendations, and a thumbnail through its Vercel functions. These requests are not made using your Roblox OAuth access token. Roblox and Vercel may process request metadata under their own policies.

How information is used

Orbit uses the profile only to show which Roblox account is connected. Locally stored library entries, recent games, preferences, notes, and cached search results are used to personalize the launcher on this browser. Search text is used to retrieve matching public Roblox experiences. Orbit does not currently use analytics, advertising trackers, or sell personal information.

Service providers and external links

The site is hosted by Vercel. Roblox operates OAuth sign-in and the experience pages opened by the launcher. The site also loads fonts from Google Fonts. These providers may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser request data when your browser connects to their services. Their own privacy policies govern their handling of that information. Orbit does not control third-party sites or services.

Storage, security, and retention

The session cookie is encrypted by Orbit's server and marked HttpOnly, so page scripts cannot read it. Cookies are sent only to Orbit's Roblox API routes. The session expires within seven days, and signing out clears it. Local game entries, preferences, and notes remain in this browser until removed or browser storage is cleared; signing out does not erase them. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Children and Roblox accounts

Orbit does not knowingly ask children to provide information directly. Roblox controls account authentication and any age-related requirements for Roblox accounts. Use Orbit only with permission from a parent or guardian when required by your local rules or Roblox's terms.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when Orbit's features or data practices change. The effective date above identifies the latest version. For privacy questions or requests about Orbit-held session data, use the contact channel provided by the person or organization that operates your Orbit deployment.
